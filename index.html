MVP – Secure Digital Signing & Verification

Legal Documents You Can Trust

Lawgda lets you digitally sign PDF documents using your own certificate, append a tamper-evident signature page, and verify authenticity instantly via QR code.

What We Offer
Key Features
🔒
Client-Side SHA-256 Hashing
Documents are hashed in the browser using the Web Crypto API. Original bytes never leave your device unprocessed.
🛡️
AES-256-GCM Key Management
Your BYOC private key is encrypted with AES-256-GCM + HKDF before storage. Plaintext keys never persist.
✍️
RSA-SHA256 Digital Signatures
Bring Your Own Certificate (.p12 / PKCS#12). The backend signs using your KMS-protected private key.
📄
Appended Certificate Model
A separate signature page with QR code is appended to your PDF. The original document is never modified.
🔍
Cryptographic Verification
Re-upload the original to verify: re-hash, compare against stored hash, validate RSA signature.
⚖️
Regulatory Compliance
Designed to adhere to REE, RA 8792 (E-Commerce Act), and RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act) – Privacy by Design.
Process
How It Works
01
Upload PDF Document
Select your PDF. The browser instantly computes the SHA-256 hash client-side using the Web Crypto API.
02
Upload Your Certificate (BYOC)
Provide your .p12 certificate. Lawgda encrypts your private key with AES-256-GCM and stores only the ciphertext.
03
Sign Document
The backend retrieves and decrypts your key in-memory only, RSA-signs the document hash, and generates the combined PDF.
04
Download Signed Document
Receive the original PDF with an appended signature page containing metadata and a QR code linking to the verification URL.
05
Verify Authenticity
Anyone can scan the QR code or re-upload the original file to cryptographically verify its integrity.
